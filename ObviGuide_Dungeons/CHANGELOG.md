# Obvi Guide: Dungeons

## Unreleased

- New: quest guides for every dungeon up to Stratholme, for Alliance and Horde: Ragefire Chasm, Shadowfang Keep,
  Excavation Site, Blackfathom Deeps, The Stockade, City of Dalaran, Razorfen Kraul, Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery,
  Razorfen Downs, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, The Temple of Atal'Hakkar, Blackrock Depths, Dire Maul, Lower and
  Upper Blackrock Spire, Scholomance and Stratholme, and Wailing Caverns. The Hall of Thanes and Ruins of Lordaeron now
  have Horde guides too. Each guide leads to the next one by level.
- New: **Dungeon keys**: Tome of Dalaran, Dalaran Sewer Key, The Scarlet Key, Shadowforge Key and Skeleton Key.
- The guides are now called "The Deadmines: Quests" and so on.
- The Deadmines: the Rough Dynamite for A Dynamite Plan is a step of its own; the Miners' Union Cards are a side task
  while you look for Foreman Thistlenettle (with the miners that drop them), and a step of their own if he dies first.
- Steps that collect drops from a few kinds of mob name them to kill: the murlocs for the Longshore Murloc Gills, the
  Sunscale Scytheclaws for the Intact Raptor Horns and the Evolving Ectoplasms for the Wailing Essence.
