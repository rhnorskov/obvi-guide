# Obvi Guide: Dungeons

## Unreleased

- Gnomeregan: the phials have a key for the troggs and slimes to use them on, the troggs and lepers that drop the
  White Punch Card have keys, and each Matrix Punchograph is a line with the interact key. The Grime-Encrusted Ring
  and The Sparklematic 5200! show only once you have the ring or the object, and the notes are shorter. Stratholme:
  Egan's Blaster the same way, with keys for the citizens and the Restless Soul.
- New: The Drowned City, Alliance and Horde: the four tablets, the Saltseers for Krazek, the Ancient Gurubashi Idol,
  and for the Horde Kin'weelay's chain to the dungeon and The Only Good Brinescale...
- The Temple of Atal'Hakkar: the level-50 class quests' parts in the temple, once you're on them: the Voodoo
  Feathers, Morphaz's drops and the druids' Putrid Vine.
- Dire Maul: the class books and Nostro's Compendium, Arcane Refreshment for mages, and the paladin and warlock mount
  quests' parts inside. Scholomance: Judgment and Redemption, The Darkreaver Menace and Imp Delivery.
- The class quests' parts in The Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep, Scarlet Monastery, Uldaman and Zul'Farrak: the paladins'
  weapon parts (now also a Deadmines guide for Horde paladins, and a Shadowfang Keep guide for Alliance paladins,
  warlocks and rogues), the warlocks' Large Soran'ruk Fragment, the rogues' Horn of Xelthos, Eye of Bhossca and
  Talisman of Kazdor, and the mages' Rituals of Power, Power in Uldaman and A Lesson in Literacy.
- Blackfathom Deeps: shorter notes, only what the arrow can't show you. Paladins get the Kor Gem for their class
  weapon (Seeking the Kor Gem, Alliance and Horde), and warlocks the Soran'ruk Fragments, once on those quests.

## v0.5.0

- WoW Forever only: retail is no longer supported.
- New: quest guides for every dungeon up to Stratholme, for Alliance and Horde: Ragefire Chasm, Shadowfang Keep,
  Excavation Site, Blackfathom Deeps, The Stockade, City of Dalaran, Razorfen Kraul, Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery,
  Razorfen Downs, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, The Temple of Atal'Hakkar, Blackrock Depths, Dire Maul, Lower and
  Upper Blackrock Spire, Scholomance and Stratholme, and Wailing Caverns. The Hall of Thanes and Ruins of Lordaeron now
  have Horde guides too. Each guide leads to the next one by level.
- The quests DungeonJournal lists that Attune misses are in the guides now: The Corruption Abroad (Blackfathom Deeps),
  The Fury Runs Deep (The Stockade), Prehistoric Prism and Earthen Echo (Excavation Site), Data Rescue, The Only Cure
  is More Green Glow, The Sparklematic 5200!, Gnome Improvement and Nogg's Ring Redo (Gnomeregan), Past Due and Final
  Passage (Scarlet Monastery), Extinguishing the Idol, The Coldbringer's Eye and Agamaggan's Blood (Razorfen Downs),
  Agmond's Fate, the rest of the necklace chain and The Platinum Discs (Uldaman), The Brassbolts Brothers, Tran'rek and
  Zum'rah's Head (Zul'Farrak), Rot and Regret and An'she's Radiance (Maraudon), Haze of Evil and Zapper Fuel (The
  Temple of Atal'Hakkar), Jail Break!, Operation: Death to Angerforge, Fragment of the Firelands, Yuka Screwspigot,
  Vivian Lagrave, The Spectral Chalice, A Binding Contract and The Challenge (Blackrock Depths), the Gordok quests,
  the Librams, Starfall and Fragments of the Past (Dire Maul), Dead Man's Plea (Stratholme), and the Upper Blackrock
  Spire ends of the Onyxia chains and The Demon Forge. Quests at the end of a long chain show only once you're on it.
  Dire Maul now goes East, North, then West.
- New: **Dungeon keys**: Tome of Dalaran, Dalaran Sewer Key, The Scarlet Key, Shadowforge Key and Skeleton Key.
- The guides are now called "The Deadmines: Quests" and so on.
- The Deadmines: the Rough Dynamite for A Dynamite Plan is a step of its own; the Miners' Union Cards are a side task
  while you look for Foreman Thistlenettle (with the miners that drop them), and a step of their own if he dies first.
- Steps that collect drops from a few kinds of mob name them to kill: the murlocs for the Longshore Murloc Gills, the
  Sunscale Scytheclaws for the Intact Raptor Horns and the Evolving Ectoplasms for the Wailing Essence.
