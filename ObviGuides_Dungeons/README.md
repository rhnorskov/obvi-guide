# Obvi Guide: Dungeons

Dungeon quest guides for [Obvi Guide](../ObviGuide), written in its own guide format. They show in the guide list under
**Dungeons**.

- The Hall of Thanes (13-15), Alliance
- Ruins of Lordaeron (15-17), Alliance

## Sources

The guide text is our own. Facts (quest, NPC and item ids, quest order, quest giver positions) were checked against:

- **Attune** by CixiDelmont: dungeon quest lists, prerequisites and quest giver positions.
- **QuestieDB** (Forever data): quest starters and finishers, objectives, NPC spawn positions.
- **DungeonJournal** by Exehn: dungeon entrances, the way down to Old Ironforge, the route to Lordaeron and where
  things are inside.

Thanks to their authors.
