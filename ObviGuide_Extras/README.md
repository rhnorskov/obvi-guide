# Obvi Guide: Extras

Guides for [Obvi Guide](../ObviGuide) that aren't dungeons, written in its own guide format. They show in the guide
list under **Extras**.

- **Aqual Quintessence**: the Hydraxian Waterlords' quests up to the Aqual Quintessence, for dousing the runes in
  Molten Core.
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag** (Alliance and Horde).

## Sources

The guide text is our own. Facts were checked against **Attune** by CixiDelmont (what each unlock takes) and
**QuestieDB** (Forever data: quest givers, objectives and positions). Thanks to their authors.
