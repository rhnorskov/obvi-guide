# Obvi Guide: Professions

## v0.5.0

- WoW Forever only: retail is no longer supported.
- Engineering (1-300), after wow-professions.com's Forever guide: each recipe learned at its trainer as you get to
  it and ticked off once you know it, the Thorium Widget and Thorium Tube schematics bought (Ironforge, Everlook) and
  learned by using them. Trainers and vendors are the nearest of the ones the site names (Stormwind, Ironforge,
  Kharanos, Auberdine, Darkshire, Zephras Isle). Parts you make for later (bolts, powders, tubes, the spanner...) are
  checked for before the guide moves on. Each trainer visit teaches all
  it can for the stretch ahead: the spanner, the bombs and Journeyman at 50; the scope, the powder, the gizmo and
  Expert at 125 in Tinker Town; and so on.
- Blacksmithing and First Aid are written as data too, made into guides the same way as Engineering: a step per
  range, training woven in, parts to make for later, what to collect and what to sell. Blacksmithing's Imperial Plate
  quests come before their ranges; First Aid's Expert book and two manuals are bought in one visit to Deneb Walker.
- Engineering is written as data (its route, recipes and trainers); Profession.lua makes the guide from it, working out
  the trainer visits, materials, parts to keep, purchases and totals. Journeyman is offered at 50 and needed at 75,
  Artisan offered at 200 and needed at 215. A step per range ("1-30"), done by the skill alone: its materials (for
  the points left, going down as you make them), what to make, and the parts to keep for later ranges. Training is a
  step of its own just before the range that needs it, teaching all the guide needs that can be learned by then.
  Weak Flux is part of the range that uses it, needed for the points left, and its line shows the way to the nearest
  seller. An introduction, ticked by hand, says the counts are for a point per craft (a yellow recipe needs more).
  A second side box lists what to sell: what the guide makes only for the points,
  and materials beyond what the rest of the route needs (the range you're in too); not the tools.
  A part that later ranges use gets its own step first ("Make 30 Heavy Blasting Powder"), done once they're in your
  bags; the range's step then levels to its end ("Make Whirring Bronze Gizmo to 150").
- Blacksmithing and Engineering: the trainer and vendor steps go to the nearer of Stormwind and Ironforge, not the one
  for your race.
- Blacksmithing and Engineering: a side box beside every step lists the materials the rest of the route needs (the
  parts you make left out), against what's in your bags. The totals go down as you finish each range.
- Fishing and Cooking (1-300): level both together, cooking the fish you catch as you go. It starts in your race's
  starting zone (Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil or Zephras Isle) and takes you through Nat Pagle's Artisan
  Fishing quest and Dirge's Artisan Cooking quest.
- Blacksmithing: each recipe is learned where you make it, at the trainer's anvil, and ticked off once you know it;
  the Imperial Plate plans come from Derotain's quests (taken and turned in as quest lines) and are learned by using
  them. Purchases (hammer, flux, dye) tick off once bought. 185-215 is Mithril Scale Pants (the name was wrong), and
  270-300 is Imperial Plate Boots: the leggings are learned at 300.
