# Obvi Guide: Professions

## Unreleased

- Fishing and Cooking (1-300): level both together, cooking the fish you catch as you go. It starts in your race's
  starting zone (Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil or Zephras Isle) and takes you through Nat Pagle's Artisan
  Fishing quest and Dirge's Artisan Cooking quest.
- Blacksmithing: each recipe is learned where you make it, at the trainer's anvil, and ticked off once you know it;
  the Imperial Plate plans come from Derotain's quests (taken and turned in as quest lines) and are learned by using
  them. Purchases (hammer, flux, dye) tick off once bought. 185-215 is Mithril Scale Pants (the name was wrong), and
  270-300 is Imperial Plate Boots: the leggings are learned at 300.
