# Obvi Guide: Professions

Profession leveling guides for [Obvi Guide](../ObviGuide), written in its own guide format. They show in the guide
list under **Professions**. All are for Alliance characters (trainers in Stormwind and Ironforge).

- Mining (1-300)
- Blacksmithing (1-300)
- Cooking (1-300)
- First Aid (1-300)
- Fishing and Cooking (1-300): both together, every fish you catch cooked on the way. Starts in Elwynn Forest, Dun
  Morogh, Teldrassil or Zephras Isle by race, and takes in both Artisan quests (Nat Pagle and Dirge Quikcleave)

Each step is done when you reach its skill level, so the guide moves on by itself as you skill up.
